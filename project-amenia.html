Town of Amenia, New York

A resident-first municipal website that turns dense public information into clearer navigation, practical self-service and a distinct local identity.

https://ameniany.gov/ ↗
THE PROBLEM

Municipal websites are only useful when residents can find what they came for.

Amenia had the same underlying challenge as many local-government sites: a wide range of audiences arriving for completely different reasons. I planned the experience around high-value resident tasks instead of making people understand the town’s internal structure first. Meetings, events, forms, permits, payments, news and other everyday needs were treated as primary pathways, with responsive behavior and accessibility considered alongside the visual design.

DESKTOP
THE RESULT

A custom direction that became a reference point.

I led the UX/UI from structure and hierarchy through responsive design, using a clear search-and-task-first approach while still giving Amenia a visual identity of its own. The finished site makes common actions visible immediately and keeps deeper municipal content organized underneath. The direction resonated strongly enough that later clients referenced Amenia when describing the kind of website they wanted. For me, it became a useful lesson in municipal design: clarity can be the visual idea, not something added after the visual idea.
