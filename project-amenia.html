THE PROJECT

Designing around the things residents actually come to do.

Amenia brought together the familiar complexity of municipal content with a clearer, calmer experience. I worked across UX, UI and responsive thinking to make high-value resident tasks easier to locate while still giving the town a visual identity that felt specific rather than templated.

DESKTOP
CASE STUDY

From resident needs to a reusable reference.

The Amenia direction focused on findability and resident self-service: clearer navigation, forms and permits, agendas and minutes, updates and everyday municipal information. The resulting direction became useful beyond this one project and was later referenced in conversations about the look and feel of other municipal sites.
