THE PROJECT

A website project that was really a system-design problem.

As sole designer, I was designing for more than one launch. The direction had to support a family of sixteen companies, stay easy to adapt, work responsively and still give individual brands enough room to feel like themselves. That changed the way I approached the structure from the beginning: reusable where consistency mattered, flexible where identity mattered.

THE SYSTEM

One family. Sixteen websites.

The design needed to scale across the PowerGrid family without turning every company into a clone. I created a reusable structure that could adapt to each company's logo, typography, colors and imagery while preserving a shared standard for layout, interaction and responsive behavior. The first variation was explored directly on staging, which made the work more implementation-aware from the beginning. Instead of asking the client to imagine behavior from static screens, I could share a realistic browser experience, gather feedback against something tangible, and make edits quickly. GitHub, Vercel, practical HTML/CSS and an increasingly AI-assisted workflow make that design-to-staging loop faster and more efficient.
