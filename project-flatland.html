Flatland Roofing

A full commercial website process, from sitemap and wireframes through homepage, internal pages, final UI and stakeholder iteration.

THE STARTING POINT

Before styling the website, I worked out what the website needed to say.

Flatland was a full website exercise rather than a homepage skin. I began with the sitemap and content structure, then moved into wireframes so page hierarchy and service journeys could be discussed before visual polish made everything feel “finished.” That gave me a clearer foundation for the homepage and internal pages and made stakeholder feedback easier to apply at the right level.

THE RESULT

The final UI came from iteration, not decoration.

Once the structure was working, I developed the responsive visual direction and refined it through extensive stakeholder comments. The finished design balances a strong home-services look with practical service information and clear conversion paths. The project reinforced a process I still use: solve hierarchy first, then use visual design to make that hierarchy feel obvious rather than trying to rescue it later.
