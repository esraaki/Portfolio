THE IDEA

A website that behaves like architecture.

Athar is a concept for a modern villa architecture studio. Instead of treating the website as a neutral gallery, I used scale, pacing, transitions and interaction to make the experience itself feel spatial and alive.

WHY AI-BUILT

No developer handoff between idea and interaction.

AI-assisted development let me experiment directly with motion and front-end behavior, test ideas quickly and publish the experience myself. The design decisions remain mine; the workflow shortens the distance between the design and the live result.
