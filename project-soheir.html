Soheir Farag Swimming Academy

A bilingual swimming-academy website I initiated myself, then designed, built, launched and continue to maintain in English and Arabic.

https://www.soheirfarag.com/ ↗
THE BEGINNING

I did not wait for a brief. I showed the client a possibility.

I already knew the academy and could see an opportunity for a stronger online presence, so I designed an initial homepage concept before there was a formal project and sent the owner a working staging link. She loved the direction. From there, we met to define what the business actually needed and I turned that first idea into the structure for the full website.

END TO END

The design had to feel natural in two languages, not translated after the fact.

I recommended the site structure, designed every page, built the site, handled hosting and launch, and still maintain it. Because the audience uses both English and Arabic, I treated RTL as a real layout mode: navigation, alignment and composition adapt with the reading direction while the visual identity stays consistent. The project became a useful reminder that initiative can be part of design work too: sometimes the first valuable thing a designer can do is make the opportunity visible.
