City of Greenwood, Wisconsin

A municipal website built within a reusable design system, customized enough to feel local while staying efficient to implement and maintain.

https://cityofgreenwood.wi.gov/ ↗
THE CHALLENGE

Reusable does not have to mean generic.

Greenwood was part of my deeper work with Town Web’s Premier system. The foundation already existed, so my job was not to invent every component again; it was to understand the client’s needs, use the system intelligently and decide where customization would have the most impact. I kept resident findability, readable navigation, accessibility and maintainability in mind while adapting variables, components, imagery and hierarchy to the city.

THE RESULT

A faster delivery model without giving up design judgment.

I carried the project from onboarding and requirements through customization, responsive design, implementation troubleshooting and launch support. Working inside a system taught me to separate the parts that benefit from consistency from the parts where a community needs its own voice. Greenwood is a good example of design maturity for me: the goal was not to prove I could redesign everything, but to make the right changes.
