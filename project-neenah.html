Town of Neenah, Wisconsin

A live municipal site shaped through system-based design, responsive thinking and close implementation support.

https://townofneenah.com/ ↗
THE STARTING POINT

Use the system as a foundation, then design for the actual town.

Neenah was a Premier project, so the challenge was not starting from an empty canvas. I began with the client’s requirements and worked out how the reusable component system should bend to fit them without losing the consistency that made the model efficient. For a broad public audience, I kept resident pathways, readable navigation, accessibility and responsive behavior at the center of the decisions.

DESKTOP
THE APPROACH

My design ownership continued into the browser.

I customized components, variables and visual direction in Figma, then stayed involved through PM and developer collaboration, troubleshooting and QA. Real municipal content quickly exposes spacing, hierarchy and component assumptions, so I used implementation feedback to refine the experience instead of considering the design “finished” at handoff.

THE RESULT

The project deepened the part of my process that sits between design and development.

Neenah launched as a live municipal site built on the shared system but shaped for its own community. Working this way made me much more conscious of browser behavior while designing and helped me get faster at spotting issues before they became development problems.
