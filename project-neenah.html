Town of Neenah, Wisconsin

A live municipal site shaped through system-based design, responsive thinking and close implementation support.

https://townofneenah.com/ ↗
THE APPROACH

Use the system as a foundation, then design for the actual town.

Neenah was another Premier project where the challenge was less about drawing a website from a blank canvas and more about making a reusable system respond to a specific client. I worked from onboarding and requirements into component and variable choices, visual customization and responsive behavior, always keeping the resident experience simple enough for a broad public audience.

DESKTOP
THE RESULT

Design ownership continued after the Figma file.

My involvement extended into PM and developer collaboration, troubleshooting, QA and launch support. That implementation work matters on municipal sites because real content quickly exposes weak assumptions in spacing, hierarchy and component behavior. Neenah helped deepen the part of my process that sits between design and development: I now think about how a system will behave in the browser while I am still designing it.
