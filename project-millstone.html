Millstone Township, New Jersey

A fully custom township website designed to pair a strong visual presence with fast, predictable access to public services.

https://millstonenj.gov/ ↗
THE APPROACH

Give the municipality character without hiding the useful stuff.

Millstone called for a custom direction, so I used the visual layer to make the township feel specific while keeping the UX grounded in familiar municipal behavior. I planned around clear navigation, scannable information and responsive access to the things residents repeatedly need. As with my other public-sector work, accessibility and ease of use mattered more than decorative interaction, especially for residents who may be older or less comfortable navigating complex websites.

THE RESULT

A custom design that made it all the way to a live public website.

I owned the UX/UI and responsive design and supported the work through implementation and launch. Seeing the design operate with real municipal content was an important final test: systems that feel clean in Figma still have to survive long titles, notices, documents and changing information. Millstone reinforced the value of designing for the messy reality of content, not the perfect placeholder version of it.
