Town of Cedarburg, Wisconsin

A custom Wisconsin municipal website shaped around clear resident pathways, approachable hierarchy and a homepage that matched the client’s vision.

https://jp0443stg.townweb.site/ ↗
THE APPROACH

Start with what residents need to understand in a few seconds.

For Cedarburg, I treated the homepage as a practical front door rather than a collection of municipal modules. The plan was to make the most important paths easy to recognize, keep navigation simple for residents who may not be highly technical, and give public information enough breathing room to scan comfortably on desktop and mobile. I used hierarchy, spacing and repeatable patterns to make a large amount of content feel calmer.

THE RESULT

The reveal confirmed the direction was landing where it needed to.

I carried the custom direction through responsive layouts and the client reveal, then refined it from feedback rather than redesigning around assumptions. During the reveal, the client said the work was “right on with what I was envisioning” and praised how the homepage captured what they had asked for. The project reinforced that municipal design does not need visual tricks to feel custom; strong listening, structure and a few deliberate visual choices can do much more.
