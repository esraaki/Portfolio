Town of Dalton, Massachusetts

A custom municipal website developed as part of a competitive RFP process, from discovery and three visual directions through a final responsive system.

THE PROJECT

A municipal RFP needed more than a prettier homepage.

The process began by understanding what the town wanted the website to communicate, what residents needed to reach quickly, and how Dalton could stand out among municipal websites in Massachusetts. After onboarding and discovery, I translated those needs into information hierarchy, responsive UX and three intentionally different visual directions. The client liked elements across the concepts, so I combined the strongest parts into the final direction shown here. My role covered UX/UI, responsive design, accessibility decisions, client presentation and revisions, component thinking and implementation-ready design.

FROM THREE DIRECTIONS TO ONE

Exploration before convergence.

Instead of jumping to one visual answer, I developed three distinct directions for the homepage.
