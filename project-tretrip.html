TreTrip

A travel website designed from start to finish across multiple service types, booking journeys and responsive experiences.

https://www.tretrip.com/ ↗
THE PROBLEM

The business offered more than “trips,” so the information architecture had to do real work.

TreTrip covered organized trips, pilgrimage programs, accommodations, events, shipping and other services. The client had ideas for what the website should contain, but the categories and user journeys still needed to become a coherent digital structure. I started by turning that breadth into a hierarchy people could understand quickly, then planned how exploratory travel content and more task-focused booking flows could live together.

MOBILE
THE RESULT

A broad travel business that still feels navigable.

I designed the project from the homepage through service pages, program details and booking experiences across desktop and mobile. One of the more distinctive ideas was an interactive program map that reveals information about destinations as users explore. The project taught me to use visual richness selectively: travel should feel exciting, but the structure underneath has to stay calm enough that people can actually choose and book.
