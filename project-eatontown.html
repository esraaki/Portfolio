Borough of Eatontown, New Jersey

A custom municipal redesign balancing a stronger visual identity with straightforward access to everyday borough information.

https://eatontownnj.com/ ↗
THE APPROACH

Make the site feel modern without making residents relearn how a town website works.

For Eatontown, I planned around the tension that shows up in public-sector design all the time: the client wants something distinctive, while residents still need familiar, low-friction paths to departments, notices, meetings and services. I used a stronger visual system and responsive composition while keeping navigation and content hierarchy deliberately straightforward.

MOBILE
THE RESULT

A more current identity built on familiar public-service patterns.

The final design gives the borough a more intentional digital presence without letting visual personality compete with usability. Carrying the system across desktop and mobile helped test whether the hierarchy was actually doing its job rather than only looking good at one size. The project strengthened my approach to custom municipal work: modernize the experience, but protect the habits that help residents find things quickly.
