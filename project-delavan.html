Town of Delavan, Wisconsin

A responsive municipal experience designed to make everyday town information easier to scan, reach and use across devices.

https://jp0497stg.townweb.site/ ↗
THE APPROACH

Design for the resident who wants an answer, not a tour of the website.

I went into Delavan with a simple priority: reduce the distance between a resident and the information they came for. Municipal audiences include people with very different levels of digital confidence, including older residents, so navigation needed to stay obvious and familiar rather than clever. I focused on clear hierarchy, readable content, strong wayfinding and responsive patterns that would hold up when the page became dense.

THE RESULT

A custom homepage that stays practical as the content grows.

The final direction balances a recognizable local identity with the day-to-day utility a town website needs. Instead of treating responsiveness as a smaller desktop layout, I considered how priorities and scanning behavior change on narrower screens. The takeaway was one I keep carrying into municipal work: when the content is complicated, the interface should become simpler.
